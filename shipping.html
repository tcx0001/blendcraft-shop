Shipping & Returns

Everything you need to know about delivery and our 30-day return policy.

Shipping

  • Order processing: Orders are processed within 1-3 business days of payment.
  • Shipping methods: We ship worldwide via tracked international shipping.
  • Delivery time: Estimated delivery is typically 7-20 business days depending on your location. Tracking is provided once your order ships.
  • Shipping cost: Free shipping on orders over $50; otherwise a flat rate is calculated at checkout based on your region.
  • Customs & duties: International orders may be subject to local customs and import duties, which are the buyer's responsibility. Please check your local rules.

Returns & Refunds

We want you to be happy with your purchase.

  • Returns: You may request a return within 30 days of delivery, provided the item is unused and in its original packaging.
  • Refunds: Once your return is received and inspected, we'll process a refund to your original payment method.

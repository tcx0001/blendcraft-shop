BlendCraft FAQ

Clear answers on fit, materials, shipping and returns.

1. Are these original OEM parts?

No. BlendCraft parts are compatible replacement parts, engineered to match OEM performance at a lower cost.

2. Are your materials food-safe?

Yes. All cups, containers and gaskets are BPA-free and food-safe.

3. Do your parts fit exactly like the original?

Yes. Every part is designed for exact-fit compatibility with the blender models listed.

4. How long does shipping take?

Most orders arrive within:

  • US: 5–10 business days
  • International: 7–15 business days

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. All orders include tracked worldwide shipping.

5. Do you offer returns?

Yes. We offer 30-day hassle-free returns.

6. What if my part doesn’t fit?

Contact us — we’ll help you find the correct compatible part or arrange an exchange.

7. How do I know which part fits my blender?

Check your blender model number (usually printed under the base). Or message us on WhatsApp — we reply within 24–48 hours.

8. Do you ship worldwide?

Yes. All orders include tracked worldwide shipping.
